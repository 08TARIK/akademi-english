5. Ne kadar süre saklıyoruz?

Hesabın açık olduğu sürece. Hesabını sildiğinde hesabın ve buluttaki tüm verilerin kalıcı olarak silinir.

6. Hesabını ve verilerini nasıl silersin?

Uygulamada Profile & Settings → Hesap & Bulut → Hesabımı sil. Silme anında gerçekleşir ve geri alınamaz. Cihazındaki verileri de aynı adımda ya da Tüm ilerlemeyi sıfırla ile silebilirsin.

Verilerine erişme, düzeltme, silme, işlemeye itiraz etme ve verilerini taşınabilir biçimde alma hakkın vardır. Uygulamada Verileri dışa aktar (JSON) ile tüm verini indirebilirsin. Diğer talepler için: nngggkklm@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy

Akademi English · Last updated: 14 October 2026

Data we process

Your learning profile (name — optional — goal, target exam/score, daily study time, interests, level), your study progress (words, answers, test results, texts you write, calendar), and — only if you create an account — your email address and password (stored only as a one-way hash) — or, if you sign in with Google, only the email address and name Google provides — plus a random device identifier used to resolve sync conflicts. We do not collect location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or payment data. There are no ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics.

Account is optional

The app works fully without an account; your data then stays on your device. An account is used only for backup and syncing between your devices.

Storage and sharing

If you turn on notifications, your push subscription (the address and keys the browser provides), your chosen reminder times and your time zone are stored with your account and used only to send reminders while the app is closed; messages travel through your browser vendor's push service and contain only the reminder text. Turning notifications off deletes the subscription. Account data is stored with our hosting provider Supabase (EU – Frankfurt, Germany) over encrypted connections; each user can access only their own record. We never sell your data or share it for marketing.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account exists. Delete your account in the app at Profile & Settings → Account & Cloud → Delete my account; this permanently deletes the account and all cloud data immediately.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export (Export data as JSON) and delete your data, and object to processing. Contact: nngggkklm@gmail.com. The app is not directed to children under 13.
